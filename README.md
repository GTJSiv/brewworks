# GT BrewWorks

A brewery operations suite that runs as a single web page: brew-day timers with a
hop and mash sequence, recipe building (malt bill, OG, IBU, ABV, SRM, water and
mash pH), yeast pitch, ABV, dilution, carbonation and cleaning-chemical
calculators.

**Open it:** https://gtjsiv.github.io/brewworks/

Works best in Chrome or Edge. It runs in your browser, needs no account and
keeps your recipes on the device you use (see Options for backups and units).
The pH, SRM and IBU figures are estimates; check pH with a meter.
